    Reading the Echo's archive

    The Echo's own back pages sit behind the British Newspaper Archive and Findmypast. Free routes in: Blue Correspondent transcribes Echo, Football Echo and Mercury reports season by season; The Everton Collection catalogues surviving Football Echo editions; current coverage lives at liverpoolecho.co.uk.

    League positions and records for 1888–89 to 2024–25 are computed from James Curley's engsoccerdata results set, with 2022–23, 2025–26 and the unfinished 2026–27 season (table as of late September 2026) taken from published tables. FA Cup runs come from the same dataset. Internationals and World Cup squads follow Wikipedia's List of Everton F.C. international players (CC BY-SA), updated to 1 July 2026, with a few obvious year errors corrected. Losses are from the club's published annual accounts. Mood is an editorial reading of each season, not a measured index.
